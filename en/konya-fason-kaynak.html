Frequently Asked Questions

Contract Welding in Konya: FAQ

Where can I get contract welding in Konya?

ArmaWeld is an EN 1090 and ISO 3834-2 certified contract welding and fabrication company based in Konya. We produce for OEMs using MIG/MAG, TIG and robotic welding.

What is the minimum order quantity for contract welding?

The minimum quantity depends on project requirements and part complexity. You can also request quotes for samples and small batches; we first agree the technical details, then carry out an FAI.

Which materials do you weld?

Our main materials are carbon steel (S235–S960), stainless steel (304, 316L, duplex), aluminium alloys (5083, 6082) and high-strength steels such as Hardox and Strenx.

Can we sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA)?

Yes. Signing an NDA to protect customers' designs and technical data is standard practice. Long-term framework agreements are also possible.

Which documents come with each delivery?

Each delivery includes a dimensional report, an NDT report (where required), a material test certificate (EN 10204 3.1) and a welding procedure conformity statement.
