Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before ordering machine frames

Which tolerance class do you work to for machine frames?

The class on your drawing. If none is given, we agree a suitable EN ISO 13920 general tolerance class with you; critical dimensions are recorded in the dimensional report.

Do you do machining?

We do not machine in-house. We leave machining allowance on the faces to be machined after welding and have them machined at partner facilities, coordinated by ArmaWeld within the same order.

How do you prevent distortion after welding?

With a tacking plan, fixtures, a balanced weld sequence and controlled heat input. Straightening is done where needed, and critical dimensions are measured after welding. More in our article on weld distortion.

Do you also build prototypes?

Yes. We build single prototypes and pre-series; for series quantities the fixture cost is quoted separately.

Do you deliver to machine builders in Europe?

Yes, with an English documentation package and on EXW Konya or DAP terms. Details on our export to Europe page.
