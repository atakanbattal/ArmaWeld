Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about welded fabrication

What is welded fabrication?

Welded fabrication means cutting and preparing steel or aluminium parts and joining them by welding into products such as chassis, frames and structures. See our article what is welded fabrication for more.

What does contract welded fabrication mean?

In contract (build-to-print) fabrication the product and the drawing are yours and the shop builds it on your behalf. For extra capacity or a second source see our build-to-print fabrication page.

Can I order from outside Turkey?

Yes. From our shop in Konya we have built parts for companies in Austria, Germany, Luxembourg and Norway. Delivery terms are agreed at the quotation stage.

Which materials do you work with?

Structural steels from S235 to S960QL, high-strength and wear-resistant steels such as Strenx and HARDOX, 304 and 316L stainless steel, and 5083 and 6082 aluminium.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

There is no fixed minimum. Prototypes, samples and small batches are welcome; the unit cost falls as quantities rise.

What should I send for a quote?

A drawing, material, quantity and any standard requirements are enough; the quote follows within 48 hours. See our RFQ checklist for details.
