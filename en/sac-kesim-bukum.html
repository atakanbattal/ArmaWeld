Process

How We Work

01

Drawing and Cutting Plan

Your DXF/STEP file is reviewed for manufacturability; cutting plan, bending sequence and weld preparation are prepared by ArmaWeld.

02

Material and Cutting

Plate is sourced with 3.1 certificates; laser or plasma cutting is done at a partner facility to ArmaWeld's cutting plan.

03

Bending and Incoming Inspection

Bent parts arrive at ArmaWeld; angle, dimension and surface are checked, and non-conforming parts never reach welding.

04

Welding, Inspection and Delivery

Welding to approved WPS, NDT and dimensional report; painting and assembly if required. The part is delivered assembly-ready with its document file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cutting and Bending FAQ

Are laser cutting and bending done at ArmaWeld itself?

Cutting and bending are carried out at partner facilities that ArmaWeld selects and audits, to our own specification; the cutting plan, material sourcing and incoming inspection are ArmaWeld's. Welding, NDT and assembly are done in-house, and ArmaWeld is accountable for the quality of the whole chain.

Can I order cutting only or bending only?

Yes, it can be quoted. The real advantage, however, is planning cutting, bending and welding in one order: one price, one lead time and parts ready to assemble.

Which file formats do you accept?

DXF, DWG, STEP and PDF. For parts without drawings, we measure the sample and prepare the shop drawing.

How are bending tolerances checked?

Every bent part is checked for angle and dimensions at ArmaWeld's incoming inspection; readings taken with calibrated instruments are recorded under the serial number. Non-conforming parts are not welded.

What do you need for a quote?

Technical drawing (DXF/DWG/STEP/PDF), material grade and thickness, quantity, required surface treatment and, if any, quality class and NDT requirements. Quote within 48 hours.
