Mission & Vision

Two statements that define who we are.

Our Mission

Every weld backed by an engineer.

ArmaWeld is a metalworking facility founded by welding and mechanical engineers. We review your drawing together at quotation stage, produce to approved WPS and maintain full traceability from raw material to shipment. We deliver not just steel — but a documented engineering output you can rely on in the field.

Our Vision

A brand that grows through consistency and earns trust.

We aim to be the manufacturer people say "if it came from ArmaWeld, the dossier is complete." Serving customers from Europe to the Middle East under EN 1090, ISO 3834 and industry standards — with the same discipline every time — is what defines our future.

We deliver not just the product, but traceable quality documentation.

Metalworking is a science driven by mathematics and physics, even if invisible to the eye. The strength of a weld emerges where the right current, the right gas, the right preheat and the right hand all meet.

Many workshops deliver only the product. We deliver the product itself, its production history and its quality records all at once.

Because we believe in one thing: the only guarantee that a critical part will operate safely in the field tomorrow is the documentation we leave behind today.

03 / Values

PRNC — 001 / 004

The four core principles we never compromise on.

Traceable production is non-negotiable.

Every part's raw material, welder, WPS and NDT record are linked. We don't produce parts without serial numbers.

Engineering decisions are made on the production floor.

Every work order goes through engineer approval. Where problems arise, our engineers who can put on the welding mask and stand at the arc make the decision.

An undocumented weld is treated as if it was never made.

Uncertified welder, unapproved WPS, unreported NDT — unacceptable to us. Paper is as serious as steel.

Errors are not hidden — they are recorded.

Non-conformance is not irresponsibility — it's a learning opportunity. With our CAPA system, every non-conformance is closed with root cause analysis.
