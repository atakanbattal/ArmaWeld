ISO 14732 Welding Operator Qualification: Competence in Mechanized and Automatic Welding

2026-01-23 6 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

In manual welding, the welder's competence is certified to ISO 9606. In mechanized and automatic welding, where the torch travels on a carriage, column-and-boom or tractor, the person doing the job is not a welder but a welding operator, qualified to EN ISO 14732. Submerged arc welding (SAW), mechanized MAG, orbital TIG and similar processes fall under this standard.

Welder vs Operator

AspectWelder (ISO 9606)Operator (ISO 14732)
Torch movementManualMechanical or automatic
Decisive skillManual dexterity, positionMachine set-up, parameter selection, monitoring and correction
Range limitsMaterial group, thickness, diameter, position, consumableProcess, equipment type, control system, single or multi-run, seam tracking
Typical exampleMAG fillet weld, TIG pipe rootSubmerged arc butt weld, mechanized long seam

For an operator, what is assessed is not thickness or position but the ability to set up the machine correctly and manage parameters throughout the weld.

Four Qualification Routes

ISO 14732 allows competence to be demonstrated in four ways:

  1. During the WPQR test: the operator who welds the procedure qualification test (ISO 15614) is qualified by the same test.
  2. Pre-production test: a test piece representing the production part is welded and examined.
  3. Standard test piece: a piece similar to those in ISO 9606 or ISO 15614 is welded.
  4. Production weld test: competence is shown by examining actual production welds (for example by NDT).

In addition, the operator's knowledge of welding technology and the machine, and functional knowledge of parameter setting, defect recognition and correction, must be verified.

What the Qualification Covers

  • Process: for example 121 (single-wire SAW) and other subgroup 12 processes may be assessed separately.
  • Control system: range changes between mechanized and automatic welding and with or without remote visual control.
  • Seam tracking: with or without automatic tracking.
  • Single or multi-run: an operator qualified on single-run cannot do multi-run; the reverse is allowed.
  • Auxiliary systems such as arc sensing or laser tracking

Validity and Prolongation

Validity follows logic similar to ISO 9606. The welding coordinator or inspector must confirm every six months that the operator has worked continuously within the range. The standard offers three options for prolongation: periodic re-testing, verification through inspection records of production welds from the last six months, or, under defined conditions, ongoing validity based on regular production checks. If the operator does not work in the process for more than six months, the qualification lapses.

Why the Operator Matters in SAW

In submerged arc welding the arc is not visible; the operator manages the weld through current, voltage, travel speed and wire feed readings and the bead shape. Correct seam tracking, flux depth, run-on and run-off tabs on long seams and inter-pass cleaning are all the operator's job. On thick plate and long seams, one parameter error can mean metres of weld to rework.

EN 1090 and ISO 3834

EN 1090-2 requires personnel performing mechanized and automatic welding to be qualified to ISO 14732. ISO 3834-2 sets the same competence requirement for welders and operators. A supplier claiming SAW capability should back it with ISO 14732 qualifications for its operators.

What Should a Buyer Check?

  1. Is there an ISO 14732 qualification for welds made mechanically or automatically?
  2. Do the process and equipment type on the certificate match the machine used?
  3. Are the six-monthly confirmations recorded?
  4. Is the operator's WPS backed by an approved WPQR?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a welder with ISO 9606 operate a SAW machine?

Mechanized or automatic welding requires ISO 14732 qualification. ISO 9606 covers manual welding skill, not machine operation.

Can an operator certificate be used for manual welding?

No. ISO 14732 covers machine operation only. If the same person also welds manually, a separate ISO 9606 qualification is needed.

How long is an ISO 14732 qualification valid?

Validity is maintained by six-monthly confirmations. Prolongation follows one of the routes in the standard: re-testing or production inspection records.

Does a WPQR test count as operator qualification?

The operator who welds the WPQR test can be qualified to ISO 14732 by the same test. It is one of the four routes the standard recognises.
