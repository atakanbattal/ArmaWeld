Generator and Compressor Base Frame Fabrication: What to Ask Your Supplier

2026-03-04 7 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

At first glance the base frame of a generator set, screw compressor or pump unit looks like a simple frame of sections. In reality it carries the engine-to-alternator alignment, manages vibration, often contains a fuel tank and is lifted by crane to site. Frame defects show up in the field as misalignment, vibration, cracks or fuel leaks.

Frame Types

TypeCharacteristics
Section frame (base frame)Welded from channels or I-sections; simple and economical
Bent sheet frameFrom press-braked sheet beams; light, common for compact sets
Fuel tank basePart of the frame welded closed as a fuel tank; leak tightness essential
SkidWith runners, lifting and forklift points; transportable on site

Critical Technical Points

  • Flatness of mounting faces: engine and alternator feet must lie in one plane. Distortion after welding ruins alignment; mounting pads can be machined after welding if needed (see machining after welding).
  • Stiffness and vibration: the frame's natural frequency must not coincide with running speed. Cross members and stiffeners matter for this, and anti-vibration mount points must be solid.
  • Fatigue: under vibration, weld ends and bracket corners can become crack initiation points. Choose weld locations and details accordingly (see fatigue).
  • Lifting points: lifting lugs or tubes must be placed according to total weight and centre of gravity, and verified by calculation.
  • Tolerances: state the ISO 13920 class for general dimensions on the drawing and define critical hole and pad dimensions separately (see ISO 13920).

Extra Requirements for Fuel Tank Bases

An integrated fuel tank needs full-penetration, continuous welds, internal baffles, and fill, vent, level and drain connections. Every tank must be leak tested; air pressure with a bubble test or a water test are common methods. Some applications require a bund around the tank (double wall or drip tray). Internal cleanliness and corrosion protection should also be agreed from the start.

Surface Protection

For sets running outdoors, the frame is protected either by hot-dip galvanising or by a paint system chosen for the right corrosivity category. If it will be galvanised, vent and drain holes must be added in closed sections (see hot-dip galvanising).

Information Needed for a Quote

  • Frame drawing or 3D model, equipment weights and centre of gravity.
  • Material, critical dimensions and tolerances.
  • For a fuel tank: volume, connections and test method.
  • Surface treatment and colour.
  • Annual volume, batch size and packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most critical dimension on a generator base frame?

That engine and alternator mounting faces lie in one plane; distortion causes misalignment and vibration.

How is a fuel tank base tested?

Usually with a bubble test under air pressure or a water leak test.

Should a generator frame be galvanised or painted?

It depends on the environment; outdoors, galvanising or a paint system for a high corrosivity category is chosen.

What information is needed to quote a base frame?

Drawing, equipment weights, material, critical dimensions, tank details, surface treatment and volume.
