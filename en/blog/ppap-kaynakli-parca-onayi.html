What Is PPAP? The Production Part Approval Process for Welded Parts

2026-01-19 8 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) originated in the automotive industry and is now also used in construction machinery, agricultural machinery, trailers and appliances. Its purpose is to prove that the supplier understands customer requirements and can meet them consistently in series production. Unlike first article inspection (FAI), it approves the production process, not just the first part.

When Is PPAP Required?

  • New part or new supplier
  • Drawing or specification change
  • Change of material, sub-supplier or production site
  • New tooling, fixture or equipment
  • Restart of a part not produced for a long time (typically 12 months)

What Is Specific to Welded Parts

  • WPS and welder qualification: the WPSs and welder certificates used in series production go into the PPAP file. A parameter change can trigger a new PPAP.
  • Macro cut-and-etch test: sections through critical welds show penetration, throat and fusion. In automotive, this test is repeated at set intervals throughout production.
  • Fixtures: series repeatability rests on the welding fixture. Fixture verification and maintenance belong in the control plan.
  • Distortion: post-weld change of critical dimensions shows up in the Cpk study. Capability cannot be reached until the welding sequence is fixed.
  • Surface treatment: if painting or galvanizing is done by a sub-supplier, that supplier's process approval is part of the chain.

Welding Risks in the PFMEA

A good PFMEA covers typical welding failure modes: missing weld, weld in the wrong position, undersized throat, lack of fusion, cracks, porosity, excessive spatter, distortion and wrong material. Each needs a prevention control (fixture, poka-yoke, WPS) and a detection control (VT, gauge, macro test).

PPAP vs FAI

FAI verifies that a single part conforms to the drawing. PPAP proves that the process can produce conforming parts consistently at a given rate. Outside automotive, buyers often start with FAI and ask for a PPAP-like approval as volumes grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PPAP only used in automotive?

No. It started there, but construction and agricultural machinery, trailer, appliance and electrical equipment makers also ask for PPAP or similar processes.

What is a PSW?

The Part Submission Warrant is the summary document in which the supplier declares and signs that the part meets all requirements. Customer approval is given on the PSW.

Does a welding parameter change require a new PPAP?

The customer must be notified. Depending on the impact, the customer may request a new submission. WPS changes should therefore be controlled.

How many samples are produced for PPAP?

Under series conditions, typically at least one hour or 300 pieces of production. For large welded parts the quantity is agreed with the customer.
