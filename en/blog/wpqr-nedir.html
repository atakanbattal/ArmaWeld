What is WPQR? Welding Procedure Qualification Records

2026-05-02 9 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

A WPQR (Welding Procedure Qualification Record) is a document certified by test results that proves a welding procedure delivers the required mechanical properties. A WPS (Welding Procedure Specification) only defines how to weld; the WPQR proves that the procedure actually achieves the desired properties.

Fundamental rule: Companies manufacturing under EN ISO 3834-2 and EN 1090-2 must link every WPS to a valid WPQR. Welding with an unqualified procedure is not acceptable.

Difference Between WPS and WPQR

DocumentContentPurpose
WPSCurrent, voltage, gas, electrode, pass sequence, preheatInstructions to the welder on the shop floor
WPQRTest coupon results: tensile, bend, hardness, Charpy, macroProve validity of the procedure
CertificateSignature of independent inspection body (TÜV, BV, DNV)Third-party approval

EN ISO 15614-1: Qualification Process for Steel

Welding procedure qualification for steel is performed under EN ISO 15614-1. The process involves:

  1. Prepare pWPS (preliminary WPS): Draft welding parameters.
  2. Weld the test piece: Using production equipment and personnel under workshop conditions.
  3. Non-destructive examination (NDE): Visual (VT), penetrant (PT), ultrasonic (UT) or radiographic (RT).
  4. Destructive tests: Tensile, bend, hardness survey, Charpy impact, macro examination.
  5. Write and approve WPQR: All results recorded; if acceptable, WPS is formally qualified.

Scope of Qualification

A WPS qualified through a WPQR covers a limited range. For example:

  • Material group: Per CEN ISO/TR 15608 group number (1.1, 1.2, 3, 8.1, etc.)
  • Thickness: 0.5 to 2 times the test coupon thickness
  • Diameter (pipe): All diameters if test diameter ≥ 25 mm
  • Position: Same or easier positions within the group
  • Consumables: Same type and strength class

Note: EN ISO 15614-1 (2017 revision) separated aluminium and nickel alloys. Use EN ISO 15614-2 for aluminium welding.

Validity of a WPQR

A WPQR qualified under EN ISO 15614 is indefinitely valid — no renewal required. However, re-qualification is needed if:

  • The welding process changes (e.g. MAG to TIG)
  • The material group changes
  • Thickness exceeds the qualified range
  • Consumables change significantly
