Hydraulic Oil Tank and Fuel Tank Fabrication: Leak Tightness, Internal Cleanliness and Connections

2026-07-29 6 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

The oil tank of a hydraulic power unit, excavator or tractor looks like a simple sheet metal box. Yet it is one of the welded parts that generates the most field complaints: oil weeping from a pore, a spatter ball reaching the pump, a bracket cracking under vibration. Ask a supplier about each of these separately.

Design Elements

  • Baffles: separate return oil from suction and reduce foaming and sloshing. Specify whether baffle welds are continuous or intermittent.
  • Flanges, bosses and threaded ports: suction, return, level gauge, breather and drain. Welding heat can damage threads; protect them or check after welding.
  • Cleaning cover: a bolted cover for cleaning and maintenance; flatness of the gasket face matters.
  • Mounting brackets: the tank is mounted under vibration; bracket weld ends are fatigue crack starters.

Leak Tightness

Tank seams are usually continuous fillet or butt welds from outside. Porosity and crater cracks are the commonest leak causes; avoid starting and stopping welds at corners. Every tank should be leak tested before delivery, typically with low-pressure air and a bubble test or a method defined by the customer, and the result recorded.

Internal Cleanliness

Contamination directly shortens pump and valve life. No spatter, slag or scale should remain inside. Depending on the application, the interior is blasted, pickled or given an oil-resistant lining, then sealed against contamination. Stainless tanks also need heat tint removal (see cleaning stainless welds).

Fuel Tanks

The same tightness and cleanliness expectations apply. Type approval requirements for tanks fitted to road vehicles (e.g. UN R34) are the vehicle manufacturer's matter; the fabricator builds exactly to the approved design.

Sourcing Checklist

  • Material, thickness and internal surface treatment
  • Leak test method and pressure
  • Port types and thread standard
  • External paint and internal lining
  • Port sealing and packing
