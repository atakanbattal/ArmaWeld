What Is EN 1090-1 Factory Production Control (FPC) and What Should Buyers Check?

2025-12-01 9 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

When a steel fabricator says it is "EN 1090 certified", it means a notified body has audited and certified the company's factory production control (FPC). But scope, validity and the highest execution class covered differ from company to company. This article explains what EN 1090-1 requires, what an FPC system contains and how to read the certificate as a buyer.

EN 1090-1 vs EN 1090-2

The two parts are often confused:

  • EN 1090-1: covers conformity assessment and CE marking of structural steel and aluminium components. It is a harmonised standard under the Construction Products Regulation (EU 305/2011, CPR). It answers "how do I prove the product conforms?"
  • EN 1090-2: gives the technical requirements for fabrication and erection of steel structures: welding, cutting, tolerances, NDT, surface preparation. It answers "how do I make it?" The aluminium counterpart is EN 1090-3.

FPC is the core element of EN 1090-1. The manufacturer runs a control system that continuously ensures its products conform to EN 1090-2 and to the declared performance.

System 2+: Who Does What?

Structural steel components fall under assessment system 2+. Tasks are split as follows:

TaskResponsible
Determination of product type (type testing or calculation)Manufacturer
Setting up and running factory production controlManufacturer
Initial inspection of the plant and FPCNotified body
Continuous surveillance and assessment of FPCNotified body
Declaration of Performance (DoP) and CE markingManufacturer

The notified body certifies the FPC; it does not approve each product. The manufacturer declares, on its own responsibility, that the product achieves the declared performance.

What Does an FPC System Cover?

Under EN 1090-1, FPC must cover the following with documented procedures:

  • Personnel: job descriptions, competence records, appointment of the welding coordinator.
  • Equipment: maintenance, calibration and records of production and measuring equipment.
  • Structural design: checking of calculations and drawings if the manufacturer also designs.
  • Constituent products: incoming inspection, inspection documents and traceability.
  • Component specification: execution class, tolerance class and special requirements defined for each job.
  • Product evaluation: inspection plans and records for welding, dimensions, surfaces and more.
  • Non-conforming products: identification, segregation, repair or rejection and corrective action.
  • Handling, storage and packaging: rules that prevent damage and mix-ups.

A manufacturer that welds also needs a welding quality system to EN ISO 3834: at least EN ISO 3834-3 for EXC2, and EN ISO 3834-2 for EXC3 and EXC4. This is why an EN 1090 certificate usually comes with a welding certificate.

Surveillance Frequency

After the initial inspection, the notified body re-audits the FPC at regular intervals. The interval depends on the highest execution class on the certificate and on how long the certificate has been held; higher classes are audited more often. As a rule, audits are yearly in the first years, and the interval may lengthen once the system is established. If a surveillance audit is failed, the certificate can be suspended, so buyers should check its current status.

How to Read the Certificate: Buyer Checklist

  • Notified body number: the four-digit NB number. You can check in the EU NANDO database whether the body is notified under the CPR for EN 1090-1.
  • Production site address: the certificate is issued for a specific plant. Your order must be made at the address on the certificate.
  • Highest execution class: a company certified for EXC2 cannot produce EXC3 work.
  • Materials and steel grades: for example "carbon steels up to S355". High-strength or stainless steel may be outside the scope.
  • Welding processes: the processes listed on the certificate or its welding annex (for example 135, 136, 121, 141).
  • Design responsibility: some certificates only cover "fabrication to the customer's design" and exclude design services.
  • Validity and current status: issue date and last surveillance audit. If in doubt, ask the notified body for confirmation.

Declaration of Performance (DoP) and CE Label

For each CE-marked component or delivery, the manufacturer issues a Declaration of Performance. It states the product type, intended use, harmonised standard (EN 1090-1), notified body number, execution class and the declared performance characteristics, such as tolerances, weldability, fracture toughness, load-bearing capacity (if the manufacturer designs) and durability.

The CE label shows the CE logo, notified body number, manufacturer's name and address, the last two digits of the year, the DoP reference number and the standard reference. It can be placed on the product, its packaging or the accompanying documents.

Common Problems

  • The certificate covers the main plant, but the work is done in another workshop.
  • The certificate covers EXC2, but the specification requires EXC3.
  • The welding coordinator is not the person named in the certificate, or has left the company.
  • No DoP arrives with the delivery, or a generic template is sent.
  • The product is outside the scope of EN 1090-1 (a machine frame, for example) but carries a CE mark under EN 1090.

The last point matters: EN 1090-1 is for structural components used in construction works. Machine frames, vehicle parts and pressure equipment are not covered; their own legislation applies, such as the Machinery Regulation or the Pressure Equipment Directive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an FPC certificate the same as ISO 9001?

No. ISO 9001 is a general, voluntary quality management system. EN 1090-1 FPC is a product-specific production control system that is legally required for CE marking and is audited by a notified body. ISO 9001 does not replace FPC.

How long is an EN 1090 certificate valid?

It has no unlimited validity; it remains valid through regular surveillance audits by the notified body. If surveillance is not carried out or fails, the certificate can be suspended.

Can a manufacturer in Turkey CE-mark products for the EU?

Yes. The manufacturer may be outside the EU. What matters is that the FPC is certified by a notified body authorised under the CPR and that a correct Declaration of Performance is issued.

Is a separate DoP needed for every delivery?

A DoP is issued per product type. Components of the same type and performance can share one DoP, but for project-based steel structures an order-specific DoP is usually prepared.
