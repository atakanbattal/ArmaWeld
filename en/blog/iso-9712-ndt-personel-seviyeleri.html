ISO 9712 NDT Personnel Levels: The Difference Between Level 1, 2 and 3

2025-12-30 7 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

An inspection report is only worth as much as the competence of the person who wrote it. NDT personnel are certified internationally to EN ISO 9712. A supplier saying "we have NDT-certified staff" tells you little unless it says which method and which level.

What Does ISO 9712 Regulate?

The standard sets qualification and certification requirements for personnel in VT, PT, MT, UT, RT, ET and other methods. Certification is per method and per industrial sector (for example welded products, castings, forgings). A UT Level 2 certificate gives no authority for PT.

The Three Levels and Their Authority

LevelMayMay not
Level 1Set up equipment and perform tests to written instructions, record results and classify them against criteria stated in the instructionChoose the technique, interpret results independently or approve reports
Level 2Everything Level 1 does, plus select technique, interpret standards, evaluate results against acceptance criteria, write instructions, prepare and sign reports, guide Level 1 staffApprove procedures or authorise outside the method
Level 3All Level 2 authority plus writing and approving procedures, interpreting standards and specifications, selecting methods, taking part in personnel examinations-

In the workshop, daily inspection and reporting is done by Level 2 personnel. Level 3 mostly works at procedure and system level and is often a consultant or on the certification body side.

Certification Requirements

A candidate must meet three conditions:

  1. Training: a minimum number of hours by method and level. Total training for UT Level 2 (including Level 1), for example, is much longer than for PT Level 2.
  2. Industrial experience: a minimum time working in the method under supervision. Direct entry to Level 2 requires more experience.
  3. Vision test: near vision acuity and colour perception, repeated every year.

The candidate then takes general, specific and practical exams. In the practical exam, real specimens with known defects are tested and reported.

Validity and Renewal

  • A certificate is normally valid for five years.
  • After five years it is renewed: evidence of continued work and a valid vision test are required.
  • After ten years recertification is required, including a practical exam.
  • A certificate may be suspended if the holder has not worked in the method for more than a year.

Employer Authorisation

The ISO 9712 certificate shows personal competence, but the employer authorises the person in writing to test and sign reports within the company. Audits ask for this authorisation alongside the certificate.

How to Check at a Supplier

  1. Does the certificate state method, level and sector (e.g. UT 2, welded products)?
  2. Is it still valid?
  3. Is the last vision test less than a year old?
  4. Is the issuing body accredited to ISO/IEC 17024?
  5. Does the signature on the report match the certificate holder?
  6. Is there an employer authorisation?

Link to EN 1090 and ISO 3834

EN 1090-2 requires supplementary NDT (methods other than VT) to be evaluated by ISO 9712 Level 2 personnel. ISO 3834-2 requires demonstrated competence of inspection personnel. On EXC2 and higher projects, Level 2 personnel are therefore effectively mandatory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Level 1 personnel sign a report?

Level 1 may record indications but cannot evaluate results independently or approve the report. Approval is by Level 2 or Level 3 personnel.

How long is an ISO 9712 certificate valid?

Normally five years. Renewal takes place in year five and recertification with a practical exam in year ten. The vision test must be repeated annually.

Does one certificate cover several methods?

No. Certification is per method. UT, PT, MT and VT each need a separate certificate.

What is the difference between ISO 9712 and SNT-TC-1A?

ISO 9712 is based on independent third-party certification. The American SNT-TC-1A is an employer-based recommended practice. European projects usually require ISO 9712.
