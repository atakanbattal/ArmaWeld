Welded Parts in Solar Mounting Structures: Where to Weld and Where to Bolt

2026-04-22 6 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

Most ground-mounted solar plants are built with cold-formed, galvanised profiles bolted together on site. Welding seems to play a small role. Yet every PV project has parts that need welded fabrication, and they are usually the heaviest, most loaded and most project-specific parts.

Typical Parts Needing Welded Fabrication

  • Carport frames: large-span column and beam systems; column base plates and beam connections are welded.
  • Special rooftop and façade frames: supports adapted to existing buildings.
  • Tracker parts: drive brackets, bearing housings, torque tube connection pieces.
  • Column bases and anchor plates: welded base plates sitting on concrete foundations.
  • Inverter and transformer supports, cable tray brackets.

Weld or Bolt?

For mass-produced standard profiles and fast site erection, bolted joints are right. Welding repeated connection points (base plates, brackets) in the shop speeds up erection and reduces site errors. For the general logic, see welded or bolted. The rule is simple: weld in the shop, bolt on site.

Weld Details Before Galvanising

Almost all PV parts are hot-dip galvanised. So for welded parts:

  • Vent and drain holes are provided in closed box and tube sections.
  • No sealed gaps between overlapping plates, or holes are drilled.
  • Slag and spatter are fully removed; zinc will not cover slag.
  • Steel silicon content affects coating appearance.

Standards

In the EU, structural steel components fall under EN 1090; PV structures are usually assessed in EXC2 (see EN 1090 EXC2). For corrosion protection, the site's ISO 12944 category governs; coastal and agricultural (ammonia) areas can be more aggressive.

Sourcing Tips

  • Package welded parts separately from standard profile supply; they need different expertise.
  • Verify base plate hole positions together with the anchor template.
  • Include zinc thickness and test reports in delivery documents.
  • Ask for labelled shipments packed in erection sequence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is welding used in solar structures?

Standard profiles are usually bolted; carport frames, base plates and tracker brackets are welded.

Which EN 1090 class applies?

Usually EXC2; the final class depends on the project and designer.

What matters for welded parts to be galvanised?

Vent/drain holes, no sealed gaps and complete slag removal.

Weld in the shop or on site?

In the shop wherever possible; bolted erection on site.
