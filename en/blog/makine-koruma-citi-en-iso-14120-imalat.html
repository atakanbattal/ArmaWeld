Machine Guard Fencing and Guards: Fabrication and Sourcing Under EN ISO 14120

2026-06-22 6 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

A mesh panel around a production cell, a sheet guard over a conveyor or the side cover of a press may look like the "simple" parts of a machine, but they are part of its safety. The machine builder designs them and includes them in the risk assessment; the fabricator's job is to produce the dimensions, openings and strength on the drawing without deviation.

Which Standards Apply?

  • EN ISO 14120: general requirements for the design and construction of fixed and movable guards (strength, fixing, materials, visibility, etc.).
  • EN ISO 13857: safety distances preventing hazard zones being reached by upper and lower limbs; opening size and distance to the hazard are assessed together.
  • EN ISO 14119: interlocking devices for movable guards (doors).

Applying these standards and taking the machine through conformity assessment is the machine builder's responsibility (see the new Machinery Regulation). For the fabricator, what matters is not changing the values defined on the drawing.

Fabrication Points

  • Mesh opening and wire diameter: the safety distance calculation relies on them; a "similar" panel can invalidate it.
  • Bottom gap and height: the gap to the floor and overall height must match the drawing.
  • Fixing: fixed guards are usually held by fasteners that need a tool to remove; captive fasteners are sometimes required.
  • Door and interlock preparation: holes and brackets for interlock switches, hinges and stops must be in the right place.
  • Strength: frames and posts are welded to withstand the expected impact and leaning loads.

Finish and Appearance

Guard fencing is usually supplied powder coated; yellow posts with black or grey panels is a common scheme. Specify colour and coating class in the order and plan hanging points before coating.

Sourcing Checklist

  • Drawing revision, panel list and layout plan
  • Mesh type, opening and wire diameter
  • Fastener type and fixing method
  • Colour code and coating
  • Packing and labelling for site installation

Frequently Asked Questions

Which standard covers guard fencing?

General requirements EN ISO 14120, safety distances EN ISO 13857, door interlocks EN ISO 14119.

Why does the panel opening matter?

The safety distance calculation relies on it; a different panel can invalidate it.

Who is responsible for conformity?

The machine builder; the fabricator follows the drawing values.

What should the order specify?

Revision, mesh type and opening, fixing method, colour and packing.
