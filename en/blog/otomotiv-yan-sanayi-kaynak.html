Automotive Tier Supplier Welding Solutions: IATF 16949 & PPAP

2026-02-15 9 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

Automotive tier supplier welded fabrication operates under some of the world's most demanding quality and speed standards. The weld quality of structural components such as chassis, seat frames, exhaust systems and door hinges is critical for vehicle safety.

Automotive Quality System: IATF 16949

  • Process-focused quality management (PFMEA, control plan)
  • Statistical process control (SPC)
  • Measurement system analysis (MSA)
  • Production part approval (PPAP)
  • Advanced product quality planning (APQP)
  • Continuous improvement (Kaizen, 5S, 8D)

PPAP Process

PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) — consists of 18 elements:

  1. Design records
  2. Engineering change document
  3. Customer design approval
  4. FMEA (DFMEA + PFMEA)
  5. Process flow diagram
  6. Control plan
  7. Measurement system analysis studies
  8. Dimensional results
  9. Material and performance test results
  10. Initial sample inspection report
  11. Sample parts
  12. Master sample
  13. Measurement equipment
  14. Customer-approved records
  15. Warranty/contract
  16. Bulk verification
  17. Appearance approval report (AAR)
  18. Part submission warrant (PSW)

Automotive Steel Grades

  • DC04 / DC05: Cold-rolled, deep drawing — body panels
  • HX340LAD / HX420LAD: High-strength, microalloyed — chassis
  • DP600 / DP800 / DP1000: Dual-phase — structural safety components
  • USIBOR (1500MS): Hot forming — bumper, B-pillar

Robotic Welding Cell Design

  • 6-axis robot: Fanuc, Yaskawa Motoman, Kuka, ABB
  • Welding positioner: 1–2 axis, part rotates while robot is fixed
  • Fixture system: Pneumatic clamps, automatic datum points
  • Adaptive welding: Laser scanner tracks seam, parameters adjusted dynamically

Laser Welding Advantages

  • Very narrow HAZ — minimal distortion
  • High speed (5–10 m/min)
  • Low heat input (0.1–0.5 kJ/mm)
  • High visual quality
  • Easy automation (lightweight laser head)

Quality Control Methods

  • SPC: Cpk ≥ 1.33 mandatory
  • Automated visual inspection: Camera + laser line scanner
  • Crush test: Shear strength on spot welds
  • Destructive testing: Periodic samples from production line

Production speed standard: Automotive tier manufacturing operates on JIT and takt time. Typical takt time 60–90 seconds. Robotic welding is the only way to achieve this rate.
