EN ISO 14122: Dimensions for Machine Stairs, Platforms and Guardrails

2026-10-26 8 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

If someone has to climb onto a machine for maintenance, adjustment or cleaning, safe access must be designed in. In Europe the basis for this is the EN ISO 14122 series, used as a harmonised standard under machinery safety legislation. Platforms and stairs are often treated as side parts, yet they are among the first things inspectors look at.

Parts of the Standard

PartScope
EN ISO 14122-1Choice of fixed means of access (stairs, stepladder or fixed ladder)
EN ISO 14122-2Working platforms and walkways
EN ISO 14122-3Stairs, stepladders and guardrails
EN ISO 14122-4Fixed ladders

The general order of preference: access from ground level if possible, otherwise stairs, then a stepladder, and a fixed ladder only as the last option.

Stairs

  • Pitch: the preferred range for stairs is roughly 30° to 38°; steeper angles fall into the stepladder category.
  • Step formula: going (g) and rise (h) satisfy 600 mm ≤ g + 2h ≤ 660 mm. Rises must be equal within a flight.
  • Width: at least 600 mm; preferably 800 mm, and 1000 mm for frequent two-way use.
  • Headroom: enough clear height above the step nosing (typically at least 2300 mm).
  • Landings: long flights need intermediate landings; the height per flight is limited.
  • Handrail: on at least one side; on both sides for wide stairs.

Platforms and Walkways

  • Width: walkways at least 600 mm, preferably 800 mm; wider where several people pass often.
  • Clear height: at least 2100 mm above walkways.
  • Loads: design usually uses a distributed load (2 kN/m²) and a concentrated load on a small area (1.5 kN); deflection is limited (e.g. span/200 and 10 mm maximum).
  • Floor: slip-resistant; grating openings small enough to stop small objects falling, and smaller still where people work below.
  • Level changes: watch for trip hazards at small steps; keep gaps and openings limited.

Guardrails

ElementTypical dimension
Handrail heightAt least 1100 mm above the walking surface
Knee railMax 500 mm between handrail and knee rail, and between knee rail and toe board
Toe boardAt least 100 mm high, max 10 mm gap to the floor
Post spacingPreferably not over 1500 mm
Openings in the railSelf-closing gate where an access opening is needed

A guardrail is required once the fall height exceeds a set value (typically 500 mm). It must withstand a horizontal load without permanent deformation, so post base plates, weld sizes and anchoring matter as much as the profile choice.

Fixed Ladders

Rungs must be evenly spaced and slip-resistant. Above a certain height a safety cage or fall-arrest system and intermediate landings are required. At the top, guardrail extensions and a suitable entry opening are needed so a person can step safely onto the platform.

Points for Welded Fabrication

  • Modular design: platforms are split to suit the galvanising bath and transport; joints are bolted.
  • Galvanising: closed hollow sections need vent and drain holes; check bolt-hole sizes after galvanising.
  • Distortion: symmetrical sequences and fixtures on long rails and beams give straight, aligned results.
  • Sharp edges: no burrs or sharp edges on surfaces people touch.
  • Stair treads: decide the nosing profile of grating treads and the connection to stringers (welded or bolted) early.
  • Dimensional check: record rises and guardrail heights at delivery.

Note: the values here summarise the general approach of the standard. Design must follow the current edition of the standard and the machine's risk assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What guardrail height does EN ISO 14122 require?

At least 1100 mm above the walking surface, with a knee rail and a toe board at least 100 mm high.

What is the ideal pitch for machine stairs?

Roughly 30°-38°; steps should satisfy 600 ≤ g + 2h ≤ 660 mm.

What is the minimum walkway width?

At least 600 mm, preferably 800 mm; wider where traffic is frequent.

When is a fixed ladder used?

As the last option, when ground access, stairs or a stepladder are not possible.
