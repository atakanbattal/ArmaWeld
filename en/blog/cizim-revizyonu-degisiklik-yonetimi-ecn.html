Drawing Revisions and Change Management (ECN): Preventing Production to the Wrong Revision

2026-11-16 6 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

One of the most expensive errors in a supply chain is a part made perfectly, but to the old revision. Welds are clean, dimensions in tolerance, paint smooth; yet a hole has moved 20 mm on the new drawing and the part will not fit. This problem rarely starts in the shop; it is usually about how the change was communicated.

How Wrong Revisions Happen

  • A new drawing is emailed to one person; the printout in the shop stays the same.
  • The revision on the purchase order differs from the attached drawing.
  • The change is given verbally with no written record.
  • The laser program still uses the old DXF; the cutting partner was not told.

A Robust Change Process

  • Revision on the order: each order line carries part number and revision letter. On receipt, the supplier checks the drawing revision against the order.
  • Written change notice (ECN): what changed, why, from which orders it applies and what happens to old parts. Changed areas are clouded or marked on the drawing.
  • Impact assessment: the supplier reports the effect on price, material, fixtures, cutting programs, WPS and delivery date.
  • Cut-in point: agree from which batch or serial number the new revision applies, and whether parts in transit and stock are used, reworked or scrapped.
  • Withdrawing old documents: old drawings and programs in the shop, at the cutting partner and in quality control are withdrawn or marked "obsolete".
  • First article check: for significant changes, the first part of the new revision is measured and approved (see first article approval).

Traceability

Record which revision each delivered batch was made to. Revision on the delivery note and quality documents makes it easy to trace back if a problem arises in the field (see digital traceability).

Include It in the Quality Agreement

How change notices are sent, the supplier's response time and how the supplier reports its own process changes (material source, sub-suppliers, welding process) should be defined in the quality agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ECN?

An engineering change notice: it states in writing what changed, why and from when.

Most common cause of wrong-revision production?

Changes passed verbally or to one person, and old drawings not withdrawn.

What happens to old parts in stock?

The change notice should decide: use, rework or scrap.

Where should the revision appear?

On order, drawing, delivery note and quality documents.
