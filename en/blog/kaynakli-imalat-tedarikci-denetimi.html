How to Audit a Welded Fabrication Shop: A Supplier Visit Checklist

2026-11-11 8 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

The websites and certificates of welded fabrication companies look much alike. The best way to see how a shop really works is a short supplier visit before the first order. In this article we share a checklist that purchasing and quality teams can use when auditing a welding shop.

Documents to Request Before the Visit

  • EN 1090 and EN ISO 3834 certificates, with scope and expiry dates
  • List of approved WPQRs, with process, material group and thickness range
  • List of welders and their EN ISO 9606-1 certificates
  • ISO 9712 certificates of the NDT personnel
  • A sample delivery file from a past job (with customer details removed)

Reviewing documents in advance shortens the visit and tells you what to look at on site. For example, if your material and thickness are not on the WPQR list, ask about it during the visit.

Six Areas to Check in the Shop

1. Do the certificates really cover this shop?

Is the address on the certificate the site you are visiting? Does the scope include your work (process, material, execution class)? Some companies hold a certificate for another site or a narrow scope.

2. Welding coordination

Who is the responsible welding coordinator under EN ISO 14731? Who reviews drawings at the quotation stage and who approves the WPS? Ask how much time the coordinator spends on the shop floor.

3. Shop floor and welding practice

  • Is the valid WPS available at the welding station, and does the welder know which WPS applies?
  • Are there calibration or validation records for the welding machines?
  • Is there a drying oven for basic electrodes and flux, and are wire and flux stored properly?
  • Where preheat is required, how is temperature measured and recorded?
  • Are stainless and carbon steel processed in separate areas with separate tools?

4. Inspection

Who performs VT, PT, MT and UT? Are the inspectors certified to ISO 9712 Level 2? Is the test equipment calibrated? Ask for a sample NDT report and check that the acceptance criterion (for example EN ISO 5817-C) is stated on it.

5. Traceability

Pick a finished part at random and ask the shop to show its material certificate, welder and NDT report. If they can find this in a few minutes, the traceability system works.

6. Honest scope

Which operations are done in-house and which outside? It is normal for cutting, bending, painting or RT to be done by partners; what matters is that this is said openly and that the partners are controlled too.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a supplier audit take?

If the documents were reviewed in advance, auditing a single-site welding shop usually takes half a day. For critical work a second visit can be planned together with first article approval.

What if an on-site visit is not possible?

Documents, a sample delivery file and first article approval can be assessed together. Starting with a small first batch reduces the risk.

Who should carry out the audit?

Ideally someone with quality or welding knowledge goes together with the buyer. Technical questions are best judged by quality, capacity and lead time questions by purchasing.
