Pipe Spool Fabrication: From Isometric to Delivery in Prefabricated Piping

2026-05-04 7 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

Cutting and welding plant piping piece by piece on site is slow, expensive and risky for quality. So most piping is made in the shop as "spools", pre-assembled pieces of transportable size, and only the joints between spools are welded on site. A good spool supplier directly reduces site weld count and erection time.

From Isometric to Spool

  • Isometric drawing: shows each line's pipe, fittings, flanges and weld points.
  • Spool breaks: the line is divided into spools by transport size and ease of erection; each spool gets a number.
  • Field welds and allowance: field welds between spools and field fit-up allowances are marked on the isometric.
  • Weld map: each weld number is linked to welder, WPS and NDT result.

Codes and Procedures

Pressure piping is usually designed and built to EN 13480 in the EU and frequently to ASME B31.3 on international projects; if it falls under pressure equipment rules, PED requirements apply (see PED directive). Qualified WPS and WPQR are needed for each material and thickness range, and welders need the relevant qualifications (see what is a WPQR).

NDT Percentages

The inspection rate for pipe welds depends on fluid, pressure, temperature and piping class (for example 10% or 100% of butt welds). RT or UT is common on butt welds, PT/MT on branch and fillet welds (see NDT methods). Selection should be representative per welder, not random.

Testing, Cleaning and Surface

  • Pressure test: hydrostatic test per spool or per line on site.
  • Internal cleanliness: removal of slag, spatter and cutting debris; ends capped.
  • Stainless piping: pickling and passivation after welding (see pickling and passivation); contact with carbon steel avoided.
  • External surface: weld areas may be left unpainted until after testing.

Delivery Package

Each spool is delivered with material certificates (heat number traceable), weld map, NDT reports, dimensional check and test report where applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pipe spool?

A shop pre-assembled piping piece of transportable size; only spool-to-spool welds are made on site.

Which codes are used for spools?

Usually EN 13480 in the EU, often ASME B31.3 internationally; PED if pressure equipment rules apply.

How much NDT on pipe welds?

Depends on fluid, pressure, temperature and class; from 10% up to 100%.

Why leave field fit-up allowance?

To absorb site dimensional differences and adjust spools in place.
