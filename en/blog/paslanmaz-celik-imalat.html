2026-01-11 8 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

Stainless steel manufacturing for the food, pharma, beverage and chemical industries must meet hygiene, corrosion resistance and cleanability requirements. 2. Hygienic Weld Details

Food and pharma projects follow EHEDG and 3-A standards which set geometric rules for cleanability. Typical hygienic weld details:

  • Internal corner radius ≥ 3 mm
  • Smooth full-penetration pipe welds (orbital TIG)
  • Internal surface Ra ≤ 0.8 µm
  • Passivation of all surfaces (HNO3 or citric acid)
  • Complete removal of post-weld heat tint

3. Sensitization and Corrosion

Stainless steel held between 450–850°C for prolonged periods may suffer intergranular corrosion due to chromium carbide precipitation (sensitization). Mitigations:

Rule of thumb: Choose low-carbon (≤0.03%) alloys such as 304L/316L, minimize heat input, keep interpass below 150°C, and apply heat treatment when needed.
