What Is a Data Book (MDR)? What Final Documentation for Welded Parts Contains

2026-11-16 7 min read ArmaWeld Engineering

Delivering a welded part is not just about the physical part. Especially in steel structures, pressure equipment, energy and export projects, the part comes with a data book, also called an MDR (Manufacturing Data Record) or final documentation. It proves the part was made to specification and is the first thing consulted years later in a failure investigation or audit.

What Is a Data Book For?

  • It provides traceability of every step from material to final inspection.
  • It speeds up acceptance; documents are complete when the part arrives.
  • It underpins legal documents such as CE marking, DoP or declaration of conformity.
  • It provides technical evidence in warranty, damage and insurance cases.

Typical Data Book Contents

SectionContents
1. Cover and indexProject, order, part number, revision, dossier approval
2. Conformity documentsCertificate of conformity (CoC), DoP and CE label for EN 1090, declaration of conformity for PED
3. DrawingsApproved fabrication and as-built drawings, revision list
4. ITPSigned inspection and test plan
5. MaterialEN 10204 3.1 certificates, material traceability list (heat no. ↔ part no.)
6. WeldingWPS list, WPQRs, welder and operator certificates, consumable certificates
7. Weld mapNumber, WPS, welder and inspection result for each weld
8. Inspection reportsVT, PT, MT, UT, RT reports; repair and re-test records
9. Heat treatmentPWHT charts and report (if any)
10. DimensionalFinal dimensional reports
11. TestsPressure, leak or load tests (if any)
12. Surface treatmentBlasting, coating or galvanizing reports, thickness readings
13. NonconformitiesNCRs, concessions, close-out records
14. PersonnelNDT personnel certificates, welding coordinator documents
15. CalibrationCalibration certificates of measuring and test equipment used
16. ShippingPacking list, pre-shipment check record

Not every project needs every section. Contents should be defined in the contract or ITP.

The Weld Map: Backbone of the Dossier

The weld map numbers every weld on the drawing and links in a table: weld number, joint type, WPS number, welder stamp, welding date, VT result, NDT method and report number. Thanks to this table, years later you can still find who welded a seam, with which procedure, and how it was inspected.

Material Traceability

In EN 1090-2 EXC3 and EXC4, pressure equipment and many customer specifications, each part must be traceable to the heat number of the material it was cut from. This is done by marking the part number at cutting and linking it to the certificate number. The material traceability list is one of the most frequently queried sections of a data book.

When Is the Data Book Prepared?

If the data book is left to the end, missing documents are hard to find and delivery is delayed. The right way is to build it along with production: the certificate goes in when material arrives, the VT report when welding is finished, the NDT report when testing is done. When production ends, only the final review and approval remain.

Paper or Digital?

Most buyers today want the data book as a searchable PDF with section bookmarks. Some projects also want a wet-signed printed copy. In a digital dossier, use original electronic documents rather than scans where possible, and keep the file size small enough to email.

What Should a Buyer Ask For?

  1. List the data book contents at quotation stage.
  2. Specify format (PDF, bookmarks, language).
  3. State the delivery deadline: with the part or within a set number of days?
  4. Ask for a draft index for prior approval.
  5. Add the retention period to the contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are a data book and an MDR the same?

In practice, yes. MDR (Manufacturing Data Record), data book, fabrication dossier and final documentation all refer to the same set of documents. The name varies by sector.

In which language is the data book prepared?

In the language stated in the contract. European projects usually ask for English or German. Standard forms can be bilingual.

How quickly is the data book delivered?

When it is built alongside production, it can be delivered with the part. Contracts often also set a number of days after shipment.

Does every order need a data book?

No. For simple series parts, a certificate of conformity and material certificates may be enough. For project work, EN 1090 or pressure equipment, a data book is standard.
