Process

From First Enquiry to Delivery

01

Enquiry and Technical Review

Send your drawings (DWG/DXF/STEP/PDF) by e-mail or through the portal. A welding engineer defines material, WPS and NDT plan; the quote reaches you in English within 48 hours.

02

NDA, Sample and First Article Approval

The NDA is signed; if required, a sample or first article is submitted with its dimensional report and NDT. Series production starts after your approval.

03

Production and Live Tracking

Every work order opens with a serial number; material, WPS, welder and NDT records flow into the portal during production. Any delay risk is visible weeks ahead, not on shipping day.

04

Documents, Export Paperwork and Shipping

The CE DoP and document file are ready in the portal before dispatch; export paperwork is prepared by ArmaWeld. Loading in Konya for EXW, delivery at your address for DAP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions European Buyers Ask

Can steel structures made in Türkiye be used in the EU with CE marking?

Yes. Members produced under factory production control (FPC) certified by a notified body under EN 1090-1 can be placed on the EU market with a Declaration of Performance (DoP) and CE marking. ArmaWeld's FPC certificate is valid up to EXC3.

In which language are the documents?

Quotation, contract, WPS/WPQR, material certificates, NDT and dimensional reports and the CE Declaration of Performance are issued in English. The customer portal has an English interface.

Which delivery terms do you work with?

Quotes are prepared with EXW Konya and DAP (your delivery address) options. Sea shipments are planned via the port of Mersin; export paperwork is prepared by ArmaWeld.

How do I follow production remotely?

On order confirmation a portal account is opened for your company. The order's position on the 7-stage line, estimated delivery, documents, NDT reports and shipping details are visible 24/7; your purchasing, quality and engineering teams see the same record.

Do you provide data for CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)?

Material origin and EN 10204 3.1 certificates are part of every delivery file. Any additional emission data needed for the CBAM declaration on steel imports into the EU is planned together at the quotation stage. See our CBAM guide for details.

Do you also quote for small batches or prototypes?

Yes. Samples, prototypes and small batches are possible; any minimum quantity is set at the quotation stage according to project requirements. Technical agreement is reached first and series production follows first article approval.
